# OpenNGC original-source attribution

- Source: [OpenNGC](https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC) by **Mattia Verga**.
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- Original files saved on 2026-10-28: \`NGC.csv\` and \`addendum.csv\`.
- Field guide: \`NGC_guide.txt\` at the upstream OpenNGC repository. RA and Dec are J2000 sky positions; major/minor axes are angular sizes in arcminutes.
- Other upstream provenance: OpenNGC compiled coordinates and metadata from NED, SIMBAD, HyperLEDA, HEASARC and related catalogs.
- Transformation: \`scripts/science/generate-openngc.mjs\` selects 15 Messier-linked objects, translates RA/Dec to degrees and adds classroom-oriented descriptions. This is a derived OpenNGC dataset; catalog-derived fields retain CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution.
- Approximate distances are separately referenced to NASA/Hubble Messier object pages for each object. These are classroom summaries, not precision distance determinations.
- The application renders original schematic sky markers. No NASA/Hubble photographic asset is redistributed. Source pages provide the observed images and their individual image credits.
